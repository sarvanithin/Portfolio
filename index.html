Selected impact

OpenAI · Mental Health AI Evaluation Framework Funded through OpenAI’s AI and Mental Health Grant Program. We’re using ChatGPT to build an evaluation framework for mental health AI, grounded in WHO–UNICEF EQUIP, the competency standard behind 18,000+ helper assessments in 40 countries. It scores how safely and empathically ChatGPT supports people in distress across six languages (English, Spanish, Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, Mandarin, Nepali) over text and audio, and tests ChatGPT and fine-tuned models as automated raters measured against trained human raters. I engineer the framework end to end: scenario and response generation, transcription, model rating pipelines, and human–AI agreement analysis.
WHO × UNICEF · EQUIP Built tooling for EQUIP, the WHO–UNICEF platform used worldwide to train and assess mental health helpers, including the EQUIP-SU app and its validation reports. Used across 40 countries for 18,000+ competency assessments.
U.S. DOE · vehicletrends.us Built and shipped a public, DOE-funded dashboard and R package used by policymakers: 169M+ vehicle listings, 2018–2025. Showcased by GW Engineering in 2026.
Publication in peer review · JAMA Co-author on a multi-institution global mental health manuscript, currently in peer review at JAMA. Led from GW’s Center for Global Mental Health Equity (Brandon Kohrt, MD, PhD).
Awards: $36,500 $30,000 DOE-funded award (Technology Change Lab) and a $6,500 graduate fellowship from GW’s Center for Global Mental Health Equity.
